# Research disclosures

These descriptions accompany the manuscript, which remains under submission.

## Company collaboration, funding and competing interests

Authors Fangjie Wu, Wenlong Zhang and Jie Gong are employed by China Construction
Third Engineering Bureau (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. The study forms part of its funded
collaboration with Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, entitled “Research and
Application of Safety Inspection Technology for Climbing Scaffolds Based on
Rail-Mounted Cameras and AI” (基于轨道相机及AI的爬架安全检查技术研究与应用).
No company project number is supplied here because none has been verified.

The company contributors participated in problem formulation, coordinated the
inspection records and site resources, curated the data, checked the cases and
conditions of use against safety practice, and reviewed the engineering content.
The university contributors led method development, software, experiments and
analysis. The company's involvement was through its employees' contributions
described above. Xingpeng Jiang and Boran Wang declare no conflicts of interest.

The research was also funded by the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong
Province, grant 2024A1515011824. That funder had no role in study design; collection,
analysis or interpretation of data; writing the manuscript; or the decision to
publish the results. This no-involvement statement applies to the Foundation;
the company's involvement is described separately above.

## Analysis timing

The width-3 comparisons were fixed in the primary plan. The width-2 arm was fixed
after the width-3 results and the retrieval diagnostic at width 2 had been seen,
but before any width-2 end-to-end result was seen. Later revision analyses H1–H8
are post hoc and use frozen outputs. The [research-plan index](research/README.md)
groups the immutable documents by study stage; the revised manuscript's Table 3 gives
the sequence and the results available at each stage.
