# Licensing and provenance

Code and rule representation use [Apache-2.0](../LICENSE). The 500 [gold images](../data/gold_images/LICENSE)
and their [label-only gold](../reproduce/DATA_LICENSE) retain CC BY 4.0, consistent with the manuscript.
The [additional-image licence](../data/research_images/LICENSE) uses CC BY-NC 4.0 for 463 eligible
files; commercial use of those files requires separate written company permission. The 19
gold-source and 18 demonstration-source exceptions retain their existing grants. The
[combined manifest](../data/image_pool/manifest.csv) identifies the licence for every original.
The 40 [demonstration files](../examples/sample_images/LICENSE) retain their original repository
Apache-2.0 grant. Third-party ConstructionSite-10k files have separate gated CC BY-NC terms and
are not redistributed.

The company owns the source photographs. The company-authorized first author arranges their release and the repository maintainer uploads copies on his behalf. See [research disclosures](RESEARCH_DISCLOSURES.md) for ownership, employment, collaboration, funding and involvement. No private authorization forms or identifying source mappings are included in this public checkout.

## Rule-library provenance

The historical `source_quote` field stores paraphrased English requirement summaries rather than the issuing standard documents. The code/representation licence does not assign ownership of underlying standards. No GB/JGJ/Shenzhen standard document is bundled. The published per-rule provenance is in [the results index](../results/README.md#data-audit).

The provenance audit identifies `R-BHV-007` and `R-CIV-016` as derived from the library's internal-source category. Their source descriptions and provenance are recorded with the rule library.

## Image-licence scope

The additional-image licence applies to the reviewed JPEGs in `data/research_images/`. It does not extend to unblurred records, private site keys, robot frames, model weights or third-party gated data. [The data card](../data/README.md) distinguishes the frozen labelled gold, unlabelled expansion and demonstration files. Source hashes and anonymised-output hashes are separate.

The non-commercial default excludes previously published photographs even if their anonymised
copies differ. Existing grants are not withdrawn. The manuscript's benchmark licence is retained;
the additional unlabelled photographs are outside the reported evaluation. No restriction is
added to the code's Apache-2.0 licence. Ordinary copyright exceptions and the licences' own
limitations continue to apply.
